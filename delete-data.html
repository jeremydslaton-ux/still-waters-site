Delete your Still Waters data

Still Waters: Ashgrove Mystery, by Slaton Games

Still Waters has no accounts and doesn't keep your personal information on our servers. Your story is saved only on your phone, so you can delete it yourself at any time.

Delete your game data

  1. On your Android phone, open Settings > Apps > Still Waters.
  2. Tap Storage > Clear storage (or simply uninstall the game).

This permanently deletes your character name, choices, story progress, trophies, messages you typed to characters, settings and unlocked extras.

Purchases (No Ads and Ashgrove Keepsakes) belong to your Google account, not to the game data. They come back if you reinstall and sign in with the same Google account.

Anonymous choice counts

When you finish a chapter, the game may add one to shared counters showing how many players picked each option. These counts contain no name, account or device ID, so they can't be traced back to you or removed individually. To stop sending them, turn off Settings > Share my choices anonymously in the game.

Advertising and install data

  • Ad data is collected and handled by Google AdMob under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Install measurement data is collected and handled by Meta under Meta's Privacy Policy. You can manage it in your Facebook account under Settings > Your activity off Meta technologies.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
