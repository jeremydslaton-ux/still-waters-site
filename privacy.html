Still Waters Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Still Waters is a mystery game made by Jeremy Slaton. The story happens on a pretend phone inside the game. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

Information kept on your device

The name you type for your character, the choices you make, your story progress, trophies, settings and any unlocked extras are saved on your device only. Messages you type to characters stay on your device too. We do not receive any of this, except the anonymous choice counts described below. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

How other players chose

When you finish a chapter in the app for the first time, the game sends which option you picked at each key moment (for example, "asked Rosa for the photos"). Each choice only adds one to a shared counter, so the game can show what percentage of players chose each option. Nothing else is sent: no name, no account, no device ID, and nothing that links the choices to you or to each other. The counters are stored with Google Firebase. Like any web request, Google may briefly log your IP address when the counts are sent or loaded; see the Firebase privacy information.

Notifications

Some parts of the story happen in real time (for example, waiting overnight for the next morning). If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device for when the story continues. These are created on your phone and are not sent from a server. You can turn them off any time in your Android settings.

Advertising

The app shows short video ads when you choose to watch one (to skip a real-time wait or to rewind a choice), and a short full-screen ad between each set of five boards in the Farwatch puzzle demo. There are no banner ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ad software may collect your device's advertising ID, your approximate location (from your IP address), information about your device, and how you interact with the ads. This data is collected and used by Google, not by us; see how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice later. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). Buying No Ads turns ads off.

Purchases

Optional one-time purchases (No Ads and Ashgrove Keepsakes) are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google tells the app which items your Google account owns so it can unlock them, and that information stays on your device. See the Google Payments privacy notice.

Update checks

When the game starts, it may read a small file from our website to check whether a newer version is required. This request does not include any personal information, but like any web request, the hosting provider (GitHub) may log your IP address. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Still Waters is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
